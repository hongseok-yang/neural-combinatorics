\begin{abstract}
We prove the chromatic-polynomial lower bound for the house graph, NetworkX
Atlas~43, on its entire required interval.  The proof is a direct graphon
sum-of-squares identity with two rational positive-semidefinite Gram
matrices.  Its machine-readable certificate is checked coefficient by
coefficient in exact rational arithmetic.  It uses ordinary non-induced
homomorphism densities and no flag-algebra rules.
\end{abstract}

\section{Statement}

Let $H$ be the house graph and let $W$ be an arbitrary graphon of edge
density $p=t(K_2,W)$.  Its chromatic target is
\[
 \Phi_H(p)=p(2p-1)(3p^2-3p+1)
 =6p^4-9p^3+5p^2-p.
\]

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:house}
For every graphon $W$ and every $p\in[1/2,1]$,
\[
 t(H,W)\geq p(2p-1)(3p^2-3p+1).
\]
Thus Atlas~43 has positive classification.
\end{theorem}

\section{The rooted basis and the exact identity}

Use three labelled vertices $0,1,2$ and one unlabelled branch vertex $3$.
There are six possible edges on these four vertices.  For each subset $S$
of those edges let $F_S$ be the corresponding partially labelled simple
graph.  After fixing the labelled variables $x=(x_0,x_1,x_2)$, write
\[
 f_S(x)=\int_\Omega\prod_{ij\in S}W(x_i,x_j)\,\dd x_3,
\]
where $x_3$ is integrated and factors not involving vertex $3$ remain
outside that one-variable integral.  Order the $2^6=64$ subsets by their
binary edge masks and put $f(x)=(f_S(x))_S$.

Set
\[
 u=2p-1\in[0,1],\qquad
 \widehat f(x)=\binom{f(x)}{u f(x)}.
\]
The certificate file
\begin{center}
 \path{experiments/house_atlas43_rational.json}
\end{center}
contains integer matrices
\[
 F_0\in\mathbb Z^{128\times107},\qquad
 F_1\in\mathbb Z^{64\times48},
\]
a common positive integer $D=10^6$, and exact rational symmetric matrices
\[
 Z_0\in\mathbb Q^{107\times107},\qquad
 Z_1\in\mathbb Q^{48\times48}.
\]
The matrices $Z_i$ are stored sparsely as $I+C_i$.  Define
\[
 Q_0=D^{-2}F_0Z_0F_0^{\mathsf T},\qquad
 Q_1=D^{-2}F_1Z_1F_1^{\mathsf T}.
\]
The exact certificate asserts the identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sos}
 \boxed{
 t(H,W)-\Phi_H(p)
 =\int_{\Omega^3}\widehat f(x)^{\mathsf T}Q_0\widehat f(x)\,\dd x
 +u(1-u)\int_{\Omega^3}f(x)^{\mathsf T}Q_1f(x)\,\dd x.}
\end{equation}

This is an ordinary graphon identity.  Indeed,
$\int f_S(x)f_T(x)\dd x$ is the homomorphism density of the simple graph
obtained by taking two copies of $F_S,F_T$ and identifying equally labelled
vertices.  If that graph has $m$ isolated-edge components, the exact
fixed-density identity
\[
 t(G\sqcup mK_2,W)=p^m t(G,W)
\]
removes them.  Expanding $p=(1+u)/2$ leaves 91 nonzero coefficients,
and the certificate checker verifies every one of them over $\mathbb Q$.

\section{Positive semidefiniteness and exact verification}

For a symmetric matrix $Z=(z_{ij})$, strict diagonal dominance with positive
diagonal,
\[
 z_{ii}-\sum_{j\ne i}|z_{ij}|>0\quad\hbox{for every }i,
\]
implies positive definiteness.  Exact fraction arithmetic gives the minimum
row margins
\[
 \min_i\left((Z_0)_{ii}-\sum_{j\ne i}|(Z_0)_{ij}|\right)
 >0.9997,
 \qquad
 \min_i\left((Z_1)_{ii}-\sum_{j\ne i}|(Z_1)_{ij}|\right)
 =\frac{999955414399}{10^{12}}>0.
\]
Consequently $Z_0,Z_1$ are positive definite and $Q_0,Q_1$ are positive
semidefinite.

The independent checker is
\begin{center}
 \path{experiments/house_verify_rational.py}.
\end{center}
It performs the following exact checks:
\begin{enumerate}
\item NetworkX Atlas~43 has target
      $6p^4-9p^3+5p^2-p$ by its chromatic polynomial;
\item the dimensions and sparse entries of the two Gram factors are valid;
\item both diagonal-dominance margins are positive rational numbers; and
\item all 91 coefficients in \eqref{eq:sos} agree exactly after isolated
      edge components are replaced by powers of $p$.
\end{enumerate}
Running it prints
\begin{verbatim}
verified Atlas 43 exact interval SOS: equations=91 orders=[107, 48]
\end{verbatim}
and exits successfully.  No floating-point value is used by the checker.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:house}]
Both quadratic integrands on the right of \eqref{eq:sos} are nonnegative,
and $u(1-u)\geq0$ for $p\in[1/2,1]$.  Hence the right-hand side is
nonnegative, proving the theorem.
\end{proof}

\section{How the certificate was recovered}

The initial numerical SDP is singular because every balanced complete
$k$-partite graphon is an equality case at $p=1-1/k$.  Rooted evaluations
on those Turan graphons give exact rational kernel spaces of dimensions 15
and 10.  Two further six-dimensional faces have half-integral bases.  After
restricting to the exact complements of these spaces, the reduced Gram
orders are 107 and 48 and the numerical feasibility margin is
$0.0056368\ldots$.  A rational Cholesky preconditioner then converts the
final exact correction into the strictly diagonally dominant matrices above.

The discovery and audit scripts are
\begin{center}
\begin{minipage}{0.9\linewidth}
\path{experiments/house_interval_matrix_sos.py},
\path{experiments/house_turan_face.py},
\path{experiments/house_face_reduced_sos.py}, and
\path{experiments/house_rationalize_reduced.py}.
\end{minipage}
\end{center}
They are not assumptions of the proof: the JSON certificate and exact
checker contain all data needed to verify \eqref{eq:sos} and positivity.

\begin{remark}
This construction is a direct rooted quantum-graph sum of squares.  It does
not use induced flags, asymptotic flag-density multiplication, or a
flag-algebra semidefinite certificate.  Every product in \eqref{eq:sos} is
interpreted directly as an integral of graphon kernels.
\end{remark}
