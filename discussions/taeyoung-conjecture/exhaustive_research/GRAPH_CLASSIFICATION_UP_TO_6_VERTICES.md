# Classification of the homomorphism lower bound for graphs on at most six vertices

We consider every unlabeled graph $H$ on at most six vertices that is non-bipartite, has no isolated vertices, and has no isolated-edge component. There are exactly **117** such graphs.

The question is whether, for every graphon $W$ of edge density $p=t(K_2,W)$,


$$
t(H,W)\ge \Phi_H(p):=(1-p)^{v(H)}\chi_H\!\left(\frac1{1-p}\right)
$$

whenever $p\ge 1-1/(\chi(H)-1)$.

Current totals are **73 positive**, **23 negative**, and **21 open**. “Negative” means that the displayed graphon gives a strict counterexample. “Open” only records the present state of the accepted proofs and exact counterexample tests; it is not evidence that the inequality is true.

The preserved starting baseline for this research pass is **117 total = 66 positive + 23 negative + 28 open**.  The seven status changes in the current census are complete positive proofs for Atlas 126, 137, 139, 145, 148, 160, and 178; the negative set is unchanged.
